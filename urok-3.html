Задание 3.1

Образуйте наречия от данных прилагательных.

Правило: как образовать наречие

Наречие отвечает на вопрос «как?» и относится к глаголу (run quickly), прилагательному (really serious) или ко всему предложению (Fortunately, …). Обычно наречие = прилагательное + -ly: quick → quickly.

ПравилоПримеры
просто + ly, e сохраняетсяslow → slowly, rare → rarely, complete → completely
-l + ly = -lly (две l)careful → carefully, natural → naturally
-y после согласной → -ilyangry → angrily, lucky → luckily
-le → -ly (e уходит)terrible → terribly, simple → simply
-ic → -icallydramatic → dramatically (но public → publicly)
от существительного — два шагаnation → national → nationally
Исключения
  • good → well: She sings well.
  • fast, hard, late, early не меняются: He works hard.
  • hardly = «едва, почти не», lately = «в последнее время» — это другие слова.
  • day → daily (y → i).
  • friendly, lovely — прилагательные, наречий на -ly от них нет.
  • 1. absolute →
  • 2. actual →
  • 3. complete →
  • 4. day →
  • 5. definite →
  • 6. easy →
  • 7. especial →
  • 8. final →
  • 9. fluent →
  • 10. happy →
  • 11. probable →
  • 12. rare →
  • 13. real →
  • 14. regular →
  • 15. tradition (2 шага) →
  • 16. usual →
Ответы и объяснения
  • 1. absolute → absolutely — просто + ly, e сохраняется
  • 2. actual → actually — -l + ly = -lly
  • 3. complete → completely — e сохраняется
  • 4. day → daily — исключение: y → i, не dayly
  • 5. definite → definitely — definitely, не definately
  • 6. easy → easily — y → i
  • 7. especial → especially — -l + ly = -lly
  • 8. final → finally — -l + ly = -lly
  • 9. fluent → fluently — просто + ly
  • 10. happy → happily — y → i
  • 11. probable → probably — -le → -ly, не probablely
  • 12. rare → rarely — e сохраняется
  • 13. real → really — -l + ly = -lly
  • 14. regular → regularly — просто + ly
  • 15. tradition (2 шага) → traditionally — tradition → traditional → traditionally
  • 16. usual → usually — -l + ly = -lly

-ise или -ize? -ise — британский вариант, -ize — американский. На ОГЭ засчитывают оба.

  • 1. symbol →
  • 2. real →

  • 5. strength →
  • 6. simple →
  • 7. courage →
  • 8. danger →
  • 9. able →

Ответы и объяснения
  • 1. symbol → symbolise / symbolize — -ise / -ize, «символизировать»

Задание 3.3 · формат ОГЭ (29–34)

Образуйте от слов в скобках однокоренные слова так, чтобы они грамматически и лексически соответствовали тексту.

Как понять, что нужно наречие
  • Слово стоит при глаголе (is changing very …, collect …) — нужно наречие.
  • Слово стоит перед прилагательным и усиливает его (… serious, … bad) — наречие.
  • Слово стоит в начале предложения перед запятой (… , more and more people…) — наречие-вводное слово: Luckily, Surprisingly.
  • Проверь смысл: хорошая это новость или плохая? Нужна ли приставка un-?
Our planet needs help

Many people think that ecological problems are (29) (REAL) serious. Air pollution is (30) (ESPECIAL) bad in big cities. Scientists say that the climate is changing very (31) (QUICK). We should (32) (DEFINITE) use less plastic in our everyday life. (33) (FORTUNATE), more and more people sort their rubbish now. At our school, we collect old paper for recycling (34) (REGULAR).

Ответы и объяснения
  • (29) really — наречие усиливает прилагательное serious; real + ly = really
  • (30) especially — наречие перед прилагательным bad
  • (31) quickly — is changing very … — наречие при глаголе
  • (32) definitely — наречие между should и глаголом; definitely
  • (33) fortunately — наречие в начале предложения; новость хорошая, поэтому без un-
  • (34) regularly — наречие при глаголе collect

Задание 3.4

Образуйте от слов в скобках однокоренные слова так, чтобы они грамматически и лексически соответствовали тексту. Здесь нужны и глаголы, и наречия.

Подсказка
  • После must, should, can, to нужен глагол в начальной форме.
  • Если подлежащее «он, она, оно» (recycling, the town) — у глагола в настоящем времени окончание -s.
  • После am / is / are + … + by — пассив, нужна третья форма: -ed.
Can we save the planet?

Today many animal species are (1) (DANGER) by human activity. To protect them, governments must (2) (STRENGTH) environmental laws. The amount of plastic waste is growing (3) (DRAMATIC) every year. Unfortunately, few people (4) (REAL) how serious the problem is. Recycling (5) (ABLE) us to save energy and resources. That is why many schools now (6) (COURAGE) children to sort their rubbish at home.

Ответы и объяснения
  • (1) endangered — are … by — пассив: endanger + ed
  • (2) strengthen — must + глагол
  • (3) dramatically — is growing … — наречие; -ic → -ically
  • (4) realise / realize — few people (они) + глагол без -s
  • (5) enables — recycling (оно) → -s
  • (6) encourage — schools (они) + глагол без -s

Задание 3.5 · формат ОГЭ (20–28)

Преобразуйте слова в скобках так, чтобы они грамматически соответствовали тексту.

Правило: пассивный залог

В пассиве важно действие и предмет, а не тот, кто это сделал: «Школу построили в 1985 году» → The school was built in 1985.

Формула: be в нужном времени + V3 (третья форма или -ed).

ВремяПодсказкиФорма beПример
Present Simpleevery day, usually, alwaysam / is / are + V3English is spoken all over the world.
Past Simplein 1985, last year, agowas / were + V3The bridge was built in 1985.
Future Simplenext year, tomorrow, soonwill be + V3The results will be announced tomorrow.
Present Continuousnow, at the moment, Look!am / is / are being + V3The road is being repaired now.
Present Perfectsince, already, just, this yearhave / has been + V3The letter has already been sent.
Как решать
  • Найди маркер времени.
  • Поставь be в это время и согласуй с подлежащим: one tree was / trees were; it has / they have.
  • Добавь V3. Неправильные глаголы — по таблице: write → written, make → made.
  • Кто сделал — через by: written by a famous writer.

Превосходная степень: one of the + -est + существительное во множественном числе: one of the tallest buildings.

The history of our park

Our town park (20) (OPEN) in 1965. At that time, hundreds of trees (21) (PLANT) there by local schoolchildren. Today the park (22) (VISIT) by thousands of people every year. Last year a new playground (23) (BUILD) near the lake. At the moment the old fountain (24) (REPAIR). Next spring, a cycle path (25) (ADD). Since 2020, more than five hundred new trees (26) (PLANT) here. The park is one of the (27) (GREEN) places in our town, and I think it is the (28) (NICE) place to spend a weekend.

Ответы и объяснения
  • (20) was opened — in 1965 → Past Simple Passive
  • (21) were planted — trees (много), прошлое → were + V3
  • (22) is visited — every year → Present Simple Passive
  • (23) was built — last year → Past Simple Passive; build — built
  • (24) is being repaired — at the moment → is being + V3
  • (25) will be added — next spring → will be + V3
  • (26) have been planted — since 2020 → Present Perfect Passive; trees → have
  • (27) greenest — one of the + превосходная степень
  • (28) nicest — the + превосходная степень: nice + st

Задание 3.7 · Чтение

Прочитайте текст и выберите правильный ответ A, B, C или D.

Как решать задание с выбором ответа
  • Прочитай вопрос и найди в тексте нужный абзац.
  • Правильный ответ обычно передаёт мысль текста другими словами.
  • Отбрось варианты, которые противоречат тексту или говорят то, чего в тексте нет.
  • Осторожно со словами all, never, always в вариантах: они часто делают утверждение неверным.
  • Если слово из варианта есть в тексте, проверь, о том ли оно: совпадение слова ещё не значит, что ответ верный.
The town that said no to plastic

Three years ago, the small town of Greenford decided to stop selling single-use plastic. The idea came from a group of schoolchildren who had watched a documentary about plastic in the ocean. They collected 1,500 signatures and presented them to the town council.

At first, many shop owners were against the idea. They were afraid that paper bags and glass bottles would be too expensive and that customers would go shopping in the next town instead. To help them, the council paid half the cost of new packaging during the first year.

Today, most people in Greenford bring their own bags and cups. The amount of rubbish collected in the town has fallen by a third. Tourists come specially to see the "plastic-free town", and some local cafés say business is better than ever.

Not everything has been easy. Some products, such as medicines, still come in plastic, and the town has not found an alternative yet. But the children who started the campaign, who are now teenagers, say they are proud of what they have achieved.

  1. 1. Whose idea was it to stop selling plastic?
    A) The town council's
    B) The shop owners'
    C) Some schoolchildren's
    D) The tourists'
  2. 2. Why were the shop owners worried at first?
    A) They might lose customers.
    B) They had too much plastic in stock.
    C) The council wanted to close their shops.
    D) Tourists would stop coming.
  3. 3. What has happened to the amount of rubbish in the town?
    A) It has doubled.
    B) It has fallen by a third.
    C) It has stayed the same.
    D) It has fallen by half.
  4. 4. Which statement is true?
    A) All plastic has disappeared from Greenford.
    B) Medicines are still sold in plastic packaging.
    C) Local cafés have lost business.
    D) The campaign has been stopped.
Ответы и объяснения
  • 1. Whose idea was it to stop selling plastic? — C: «The idea came from a group of schoolchildren»
  • 2. Why were the shop owners worried at first? — A: «customers would go shopping in the next town instead»
  • 3. What has happened to the amount of rubbish in the town? — B: «has fallen by a third»; half в тексте — про оплату упаковки, это ловушка
  • 4. Which statement is true? — B: «Some products, such as medicines, still come in plastic»
